About the company

Fatema Civilworks & Contracting (ফাতিমা সিভিলওয়ার্কস অ্যান্ড কন্ট্রাক্টিং) is a civil construction and contracting firm in Bangladesh. We take on private, commercial and institutional work, from a single boundary wall to a complete building.

Every project gets one point of contact, a written scope and a fixed timeline, so you always know what is being built, what it costs and when it will be handed over.
